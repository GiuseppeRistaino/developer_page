Account and data deletion

This page explains how ScanTrak users can delete their account and request deletion of the related data.

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Contact for requests

Giuseppe Ristaino

Email: vrexas.developer@gmail.com

Request deletion by email

Delete from the app

  1. Open ScanTrak.
  2. Go to the Settings page.
  3. Scroll to the Account deletion section.
  4. Select Delete account.
  5. Confirm. For security, you may be asked to sign in again with your password or Google.

Request by email

If you can't access the app, send a request to vrexas.developer@gmail.com, if possible from the same email address linked to your ScanTrak account.

Suggested subject: ScanTrak account deletion. In your message, include the account email or another detail that helps identify it. If you write from a different address, we may ask for extra verification before proceeding.

Data deleted

  • User profile linked to Firebase.
  • Firebase Auth account, when it can be verified and linked to the request.
  • Receipts, items, totals, categories and images in the ScanTrak cloud backup.
  • AI counters and cloud data linked to the user.
  • Cloud preferences linked to the account.

Data kept and timing

Requests are normally handled within 30 days. Some technical or administrative data may be kept longer only when needed for legal obligations, security, abuse prevention or handling disputes.

Data stored only on your device can't be accessed remotely by the provider. You can remove it with the app's deletion feature, by clearing the app's data on your device or by uninstalling the app.

Privacy

For details on the data processed, purposes, cloud, AI, advertising and backup, see the ScanTrak Privacy Policy.
