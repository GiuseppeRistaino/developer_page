Using the app

  • ScanTrak helps you digitise receipts, read their data with AI and view personal spending statistics.
  • You are responsible for the accuracy of the data you save and can edit it before saving.
  • Free users have daily limits and must watch a rewarded ad before AI scanning.
  • Premium users get extra features such as no ads, export and optional cloud backup.

AI scanning

  • AI scanning sends receipt images to the Google Gemini API, as described in the Privacy Policy.
  • AI analysis can make mistakes: check the results before relying on them.

Cloud backup

  • Cloud backup is optional and reserved for the Premium plan. When turned on, it saves receipts and related images to Firebase.
  • Turning it off stops new uploads; to remove data already uploaded, use cloud cleanup.

Premium subscription

Premium is a monthly subscription purchased through Google Play. It renews automatically until you cancel it from Google Play; the price is shown in your local currency before you buy. Refunds and cancellations follow Google Play's policies.

Availability

Some features depend on Firebase, Google Sign-In, AdMob, an internet connection and the Google Gemini API. If these are temporarily unavailable, the app may limit some operations.

Account deletion

You can delete your account from the Settings page. Deletion removes your account, profile and related Firebase data, as well as local data on the device. This is permanent and may require you to sign in again. You can also use the account deletion page.
