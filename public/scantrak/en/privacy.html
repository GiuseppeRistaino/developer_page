Privacy Policy

ScanTrak is designed to work locally: receipts, images and statistics are stored on your device first. This page explains what data may be processed and how you stay in control.

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Data controller

Giuseppe Ristaino

Country: Italy

Privacy contact: vrexas.developer@gmail.com

Data we process

  • Account: email, display name, Firebase user ID, current plan and Premium status.
  • Receipts: store, date, total, currency, payment method, items, categories and receipt images.
  • AI usage: daily scan count and images sent to the Google Gemini API for analysis.
  • Cloud backup: data and images uploaded to Firebase only if a Premium user turns this feature on.
  • Premium purchases: purchased product ID, Google Play purchase token, expiry date and subscription status. Payment details (card, account) are handled only by Google Play and never reach ScanTrak.
  • Advertising: for Free users, Google AdMob collects the device advertising ID, app set ID, IP address (which may be used to estimate approximate location), interactions with ads and the app, and diagnostic performance data. Google UMP records your advertising consent choices.

Purposes

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Automatically extract data from receipts using AI.
  • Show your archive, dashboard and personal spending statistics.
  • Apply daily limits and Free or Premium plan features.
  • Run cloud backup if a Premium user turns it on.
  • Show rewarded ads to Free users before AI scanning.
  • Handle export, data deletion and account deletion.

Cloud, AI and providers

When you use AI scanning, receipt images are sent through ScanTrak Cloud Functions to the Google Gemini API, Google's artificial intelligence service, to extract the data. The result is returned to the app and stored mainly on your device. ScanTrak doesn't keep these images on its servers unless you turn on cloud backup.

Google processes this data under the Gemini API terms: it may keep it for a limited time to prevent abuse and may process it outside the European Union. Receipts may contain personal data: avoid photographing receipts that show information you don't want to share.

ScanTrak also uses Google Firebase (authentication, database, storage and Cloud Functions), Google Sign-In, Google Play Billing, Google AdMob and Google UMP. Cloud backup is separate from AI scanning: if a Premium user turns it on, ScanTrak saves receipts, items, totals, categories and related images to Firebase.

Your local data (receipts database, categories and preferences, but not receipt photos) may be included in your device's Android backup on your Google account. That backup is end-to-end encrypted with your screen lock and is managed by Android, not by ScanTrak.

Advertising

Free users watch a rewarded ad before each AI scan. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects the data listed above and shares it with Google to show and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud. Personalised ads are shown only if you agree through the Google UMP consent form; otherwise ads are not personalised.

You can change your consent choices from the Data and privacy section of the app, and reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Google processes this data under its own policy on how Google uses information from partner sites and apps. Premium users don't see ads.

Legal bases

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): account, AI scanning, archive, statistics, cloud backup, Premium subscription and account deletion.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): personalised ads. You can withdraw it at any time, without affecting processing carried out before.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): daily scan limits, rewarded ad verification, security and abuse prevention.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR): keeping purchase records as required by tax law.

Transfers outside the European Union

The Google Gemini API, Firebase and AdMob are Google services that may process data outside the European Union, in particular in the United States. These transfers rely on the safeguards provided by the GDPR: the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission and, for Google LLC, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions).

Retention and control

  • Local data stays on your device until you delete it from the app or uninstall the app.
  • Account data (email, name, plan) is kept for as long as the account exists and is deleted when you delete the account.
  • Cloud backup data stays until you remove it with the cloud cleanup features or delete your account.
  • The daily AI scan counter is used to apply plan limits, is linked to your account and is deleted with it.
  • Purchase data is kept for the duration of the subscription and then for as long as tax law requires.
  • Images sent to the Google Gemini API may be kept by Google for a limited time, as described above.
  • Premium users can export their local data to CSV, JSON or an image ZIP.

Account and data deletion

You can delete your account from the app or request deletion through the dedicated page. This removes your Firebase profile, Firebase Auth account, related cloud data, images in the cloud backup and AI counters linked to you. Local data on the device can be deleted from the app or by clearing the app's data on the device.

Your rights

Under Articles 15–22 of the GDPR (and the UK GDPR) you have the right to:

  • access your data and get a copy of it;
  • ask for it to be corrected or deleted;
  • ask for processing to be restricted;
  • receive your data in a structured format and transfer it elsewhere (portability);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • withdraw your consent to personalised ads at any time.

You can manage consents, cloud backup, export, data deletion and account deletion from the Data and privacy section and the Settings page of the app. To exercise your other rights, write to vrexas.developer@gmail.com: we reply within 30 days.

If you believe the processing breaches data protection law, you can complain to a supervisory authority: the Italian Garante per la protezione dei dati personali or the authority in your EU country, the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), or the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).
